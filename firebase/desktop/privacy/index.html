Mealsy privacy notice

Mealsy is provided and supported by Callum. Updated 22 October 2026. Privacy contact: Callum@brightbark.co.uk.

What Mealsy stores

Device mode stores recipes, photos, shopping lists, meal plans, settings and drafts locally. Creating an account sends your email and authentication information to Firebase Authentication. Mealsy syncs your profile, recipes, photos included with recipes, snacks, essentials and personal shopping list using Firebase services. Dietary profile details are optional and may contain sensitive information; do not supply anything you do not want stored.

Household sharing

Household members can access the household shopping list and shared meal plan, including attached recipe snapshots and notes. Your personal recipe book and personal list remain separate. Personal meal plans, reusable weeks, saved shopping trips and drafts stay on the device. Joining a household does not automatically publish personal recipes.

Service providers and purchases

Mealsy uses Google Firebase for authentication, cloud storage of app records and server functions. Google Play handles Android subscription payment details. Mealsy receives purchase tokens and verified subscription status to provide paid access; it does not collect payment-card details. Recipe importing contacts the selected recipe website when you request an import.

Diagnostics and advertising

Current versions default optional Firebase Crashlytics reporting to off when no choice was saved. Earlier versions may have enabled reporting by default; check your choice in Settings. If enabled, it can send crash, app and device information; the custom managed reporter removes raw submitted text, provider error messages and account identifiers. Change your choice in Settings. Disabling reporting does not remove reports already delivered. Current releases do not serve live advertisements or offer public community features. Advertising SDKs are included in the app. If advertising or public sharing is enabled in a future release, this notice and applicable privacy choices will be updated.

Retention and deletion

Account content is kept to provide the service until deleted or an account-deletion request is processed. Device data can remain after signing out; signing out is not deletion. To request account and associated personal-data deletion, follow the account deletion instructions. Ownership must be verified. Where automated deletion is available, an accepted request disables access, removes account content and owned shared spaces, runs a later cleanup sweep, and removes the Firebase login. A minimal deletion-job marker and random support reference are scheduled for removal after 30 days to prevent retries or stale writes from restoring data. If automated deletion is unavailable, contact support to arrange verified deletion. Legacy de-identified campaign aggregates follow their existing expiry schedules; copies other people saved into their own private accounts are separate. Limited payment, fraud-prevention or legal records may need retention; support will explain any applicable exceptions. Provider backups and already delivered diagnostics are subject to provider retention. Deletion does not automatically cancel Google Play subscriptions.

Your choices

You may use personal tools without an account, omit optional profile information, control diagnostics, edit or remove your content and request account deletion. Sharing with a household is optional. Mealsy is not a medical or nutrition-advice service. Contact support for questions, corrections, access or deletion requests.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, email Callum@brightbark.co.uk. You do not need to install the app to contact us. For information about our Firebase service provider, see Firebase privacy information.
