Delete your Mealsy account and data

Where automated deletion is available in your installed version, open Settings → Privacy, data & account deletion → Delete my account automatically. Confirm your password, review owned shared spaces, type DELETE and confirm. The server queues cleanup and removes access when accepted. Completion continues in the background; the app provides a request reference. Other devices may retain offline copies until their app data is cleared.

If automated deletion is unavailable, or you prefer not to install or sign into the app, request deletion by email:

  1. Email Callum@brightbark.co.uk with the subject Mealsy account and data deletion request.
  2. Send from your registered account email where possible, or state the registered email. Do not send passwords, payment-card details or identity documents in the initial request.
  3. Support will verify account ownership, explain any effect on an owned household and arrange deletion. We will confirm completion and explain any records that must be retained and why.

What the request covers

Request deletion of the Firebase login and associated personal profile, recipes, recipe photos stored with those records, personal shopping list, snacks and essentials. Support must also address household membership, invitations, shared content associated with the account, historic social content (where present), notifications and purchase-account links. Other members’ personal accounts and personal content are not yours to delete. Support will confirm the handling of shared household records before proceeding.

Device and subscription data

Signing out or uninstalling is not a cloud-deletion request. Device-only data and offline account caches require removal from each device; support will include instructions. Account deletion does not cancel a subscription. Manage or cancel Google Play subscriptions separately to avoid further renewal charges.

Exceptions and questions

Some billing, fraud-prevention or legally required records may need retention. Support must explain the specific records, purpose and applicable retention to you. Provider backups and already delivered diagnostics may have separate retention. Contact the same address for questions or to follow up on a request.

In the app, the same request is available under Settings → Privacy, data & account deletion. Updated 22 October 2026.
